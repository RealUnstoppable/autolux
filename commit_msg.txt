feat: Implement Digital Vehicle Service History & Before/After Gallery

This commit adds a new feature that allows admins to document detailed service logs for users' vehicles, including before/after image URLs, service types, and technician notes. These logs are then displayed to users in their client portal (`account.html`) as a premium gallery.

- Adds strict `firestore.rules` for the new `service_logs` collection.
- Creates `js/service_logs.js` to handle CRUD operations and fallback queries.
- Updates `admin.html` with a new form section to create service logs.
- Updates `account.html` to securely fetch and elegantly display the user's service history.
